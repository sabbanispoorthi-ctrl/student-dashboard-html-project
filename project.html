About SkillBridge

SkillBridge is a student-focused website designed to help students share and learn skills from one another.

Students can showcase the skills they can teach and discover other students who have the skills they want to learn.

Our aim is to encourage peer-to-peer learning, collaboration and knowledge sharing among students.
